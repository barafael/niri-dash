## Night light {#night-light}

{{#include ../widgets.generated.md:night-light}}

`niri-dash night-light` toggles it, `on` and `off` switch it, and a temperature in kelvin (1000 to 6500) turns it on at that warmth. On Plasma the night light is KWin's: the switch pauses or resumes its schedule while niri-dash runs, and its times and warmth are set in System Settings.
